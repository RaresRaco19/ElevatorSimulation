Simulator Architecture

Two pluggable layers over a fixed engine — src/elevator_sim/

A request arrives. Two separate decisions follow, and the whole design turns on keeping them apart: which car takes it, and then how that car moves. Each is a swappable layer with one method; everything else belongs to the engine.

Two layers, two questions

The “not its job” lines are the load-bearing part. Everything that passes between the layers is that one committed stop — there is no richer handover. Because neither layer reaches into the other, any scheduler runs against any car policy, and a change to one cannot quietly alter the other.

One tick, four steps

One tick is one floor of travel. Boarding is instantaneous, which is why a car's arrival time is pure geometry — the property core/trip_estimator.py is built on.

What plugs in

Adding a seventh combination means adding one file and one registry entry in run_simulation.py — nothing in core/ changes.

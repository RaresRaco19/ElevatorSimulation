LOOK Car Policy

Reverses early, no wasted sweeps — src/elevator_sim/car_policies/look.py

How it works

  • Same start as SCAN. While an elevator has committed stops, it keeps moving in its current direction, serving every stop it passes along the way. Picking a direction from idle works identically: UP if any stop is above, else DOWN if any is below, else stay IDLE.
  • The one difference: reversal rule. It reverses as soon as there is no committed stop further in the current direction — it never keeps going just to reach the building's physical top or bottom floor.
  • Result. Avoids the wasted sweeps scan.py takes when nothing is left to serve in the current direction.

Diagram

Same scenario as scan.py's diagram: idle at floor 1, committed stops at floors 3 and 4. LOOK serves both, then — with nothing left above — reverses immediately at floor 4 instead of continuing to floor 6. It reaches floor 1 by t6; the dashed line shows SCAN still climbing to the top, only reaching floor 1 by t10.

Contrast

Contrast with scan.py (true SCAN), which always runs to the physical terminal floor before reversing, regardless of remaining stops — see docs/scan.html. Measured on identical input: outputs/runs/round_robin_scan_stress/ vs round_robin_look_stress/ — max wait time drops from 90 to 54 ticks, average estimate_drift from 6.8 to 0.8.

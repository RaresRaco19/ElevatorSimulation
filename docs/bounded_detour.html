Bounded Detour Car Policy

LOOK, but it may turn back — within hard bounds — src/elevator_sim/car_policies/bounded_detour.py

Press the button one floor behind a rising car under plain LOOK and you wait out its entire remaining climb and the trip back down. Letting it turn back freely is worse — the car thrashes and everyone aboard pays, with no ceiling. So: turn back, but bound it.

The bounds

The budget refreshes on a natural reversal, not on a timer. Every quantity involved is exact rather than sampled, so the bound is directly checkable — tests/test_car_policies.py checks it against a plain LOOK baseline on the same queue.

One diversion, start to finish

A car at floor 4 climbing to a stop at 6, when a stop at 3 — one floor behind — joins its queue. It dips back, serves it, and resumes exactly where it left off.

Why one check covers everyone

A diversion is a sweep extension that snaps back instead of persisting: once the car is home, it resumes the trajectory it would have taken anyway. So every remaining stop moves by the same 2 × distance, wherever it sits. That claim is load-bearing, so tests/test_trip_estimator.py checks it against a brute-force per-passenger comparison rather than assuming it.

Measured
